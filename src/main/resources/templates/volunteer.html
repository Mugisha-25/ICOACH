Why Volunteer?

Our volunteers are the heart of Iowa Community Center. Whether you tutor students, assist with job coaching, help with driving practice, or support community events — your contribution changes lives.

In 2025, our 43 volunteers contributed over 1,255 hours of service to our community programs.
