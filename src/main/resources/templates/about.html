Who We Are

A Community Built on Hope and Opportunity

Iowa Community Center was established to be a safe, welcoming space where immigrant, refugee, and underserved families can access the resources and support they need to build stable, thriving lives. We believe that when families are strong, entire communities flourish.

From after-school tutoring to workforce training, from driving support to early childhood education, our programs meet families at every stage of their journey — with culturally responsive care, bilingual resources, and deep respect for every individual's dignity and potential.

What Guides Us

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To empower immigrant, refugee, and underserved families through accessible education, workforce development, and community support — building pathways to lasting self-sufficiency and wellbeing.

Our Vision

A community where every family — regardless of origin, language, or background — has the opportunity and support to reach their full potential and contribute to a vibrant, equitable society.

Our Values

Compassion, Equity, Cultural Respect, Innovation, Collaboration, and Accountability guide every decision we make and every program we deliver.
