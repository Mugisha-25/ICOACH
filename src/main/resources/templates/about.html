Who We Are

A Community Built on Hope and Opportunity

Iowa Community Center (ICC) is a registered nonprofit organization dedicated to empowering immigrant, refugee, and underserved families in the Des Moines area. We are a safe, welcoming space where families can access the resources and support they need to build stable, thriving lives.

From after-school tutoring to workforce training, from driving practice to early childhood education, our programs meet families at every stage of their journey — with culturally responsive care and deep respect for every individual's dignity and potential.

What Guides Us

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To empower immigrant, refugee, and underserved communities by creating opportunities, providing practical support, and building strong community connections.

Our Vision

A community where every family — regardless of origin, language, or background — has the opportunity and support to reach their full potential and contribute to a vibrant, equitable society.

Our Values

Compassion, Equity, Cultural Respect, Innovation, Collaboration, and Accountability guide every decision we make and every program we deliver.
